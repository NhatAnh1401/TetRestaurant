✦ Our Story

A Taste of Vietnam in the Heart of Hanoi's Old Quarter

Located at 29 Lan Ong, Tết Restaurant is celebrated for its rich, authentic Vietnamese flavours and a warm atmosphere that feels like home. Our name honours Vietnam's most cherished festival — a season of family, warmth, and the finest food.

Whether you're craving a bowl of slow-simmered Northern phở, savoury bánh xèo, or our popular vegetarian options, every dish is made with fresh, market-sourced ingredients and generations of culinary heritage.
