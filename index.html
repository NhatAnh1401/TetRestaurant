✦ Our Story

"Tết" — Where Every Meal Is a Celebration

Tết is Vietnam's most cherished festival — a symbol of hope, family, and the finest food. At 29 Lan Ong, we have woven that spirit into every dish we serve. Each ingredient and recipe embodies the utmost attention to detail, honouring the soul of Vietnamese culinary heritage.

With its inviting ambiance and harmonious design, Tết Restaurant offers the perfect setting to experience authentic Vietnamese cuisine and create cherished memories with your loved ones.
